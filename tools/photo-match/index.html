Photo Match

Headshots found in your Google Drive, matched to people in your org-chart directory. Approve the ones that look right, then apply — they save straight into your charts.

Privacy note: I only used images clearly named for a person (headshots & name-tagged photos). Driver's licenses, passports, SSN cards and other ID documents that surfaced in the search were deliberately excluded and never used.
